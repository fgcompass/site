Class list and QR labels

Give each child a QR label. When it's a child's turn, tap Scan in the web app and point the camera at their label. Their name fills in, so you can rate straight away. Nothing is uploaded. The list stays on this device.

  1. Make a class list with a column for each child's name or ID. Rater and location columns are optional.
  2. Upload it here, or paste the names.
  3. Print the labels and give one to each child, or stick them on a sheet.
  4. In the web app, tap Scan for each child in line.
